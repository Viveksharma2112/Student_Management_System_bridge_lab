## ✅ Everything You Need to Know

### What is this project?
A Student Result Management System that:
- Manages student records
- Stores exam results
- Calculates grades automatically
- Generates report cards
- Shows class performance

### How does it work?
1. **Frontend (React)** - What you see in the browser
2. **Backend (Express)** - Handles data operations
3. **Database (JSON)** - Stores students and results

### What can it do?
- ✅ Add/Delete students
- ✅ Enter exam results
- ✅ Calculate grades (A+ to F)
- ✅ View individual report cards
- ✅ See class summary
- ✅ Search students
- ✅ Print reports
